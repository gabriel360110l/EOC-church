# All 21 Catholic Ecumenical Councils

The Catholic Church recognizes 21 ecumenical councils, from Nicaea I (325) to Vatican II (1962–1965).

## List of the 21 Councils

| # | Council | Year | Key Topics |
|---|---------|------|------------|
| 1 | Nicaea I | 325 | Divinity of Christ, Nicene Creed, Easter date |
| 2 | Constantinople I | 381 | Divinity of the Holy Spirit, Creed completed |
| 3 | Ephesus | 431 | Mary as Theotokos, against Nestorius |
| 4 | Chalcedon | 451 | Two natures of Christ |
| 5 | Constantinople II | 553 | Against Three Chapters |
| 6 | Constantinople III | 680–681 | Against Monothelitism |
| 7 | Nicaea II | 787 | Veneration of icons |
| 8 | Constantinople IV | 869–870 | Against Photius |
| 9 | Lateran I | 1123 | Investiture controversy |
| 10 | Lateran II | 1139 | Clerical celibacy, against schism |
| 11 | Lateran III | 1179 | Papal election |
| 12 | Lateran IV | 1215 | Transubstantiation, annual confession |
| 13 | Lyon I | 1245 | Against Frederick II |
| 14 | Lyon II | 1274 | Union with Greeks |
| 15 | Vienne | 1311–1312 | Knights Templar, reform |
| 16 | Constance | 1414–1418 | End of Western Schism |
| 17 | Basel-Ferrara-Florence | 1431–1449 | Union with East, reform |
| 18 | Lateran V | 1512–1517 | Reform before Reformation |
| 19 | Trent | 1545–1563 | Against Protestantism, reform, seminaries |
| 20 | Vatican I | 1869–1870 | Papal infallibility, faith and reason |
| 21 | Vatican II | 1962–1965 | Liturgy, Church, modern world |

## Notes for the App
- Early councils (1–7) are shared with Eastern Orthodox.
- Oriental Orthodox accept the first 3.
- For Council Reading Plans, include relevant Bible passages, historical reason, key canons summary, and outcome.
- Full canons of later councils (especially Trent and Vatican II) are long. Use summaries in the app and link to full texts where possible.
