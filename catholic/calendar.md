# Catholic — Calendar & Saints

---

## calapi.inadiutorium.cz
- **What it is:** Free API that returns the Catholic liturgical day information.
- **What you get:** Feast of the day, liturgical color, rank, and celebrations.
- **Endpoint:** `http://calapi.inadiutorium.cz/api/v0/en/calendars/default/today`
- **Best for:** Simple daily Catholic calendar data.

---

## romcal / romcal
- **What it is:** Powerful JavaScript/TypeScript library that calculates the full Catholic liturgical year and local saints.
- **What you get:** Complete calendar engine, including national calendars (e.g. Dutch or Belgian saints).
- **Link:** https://github.com/romcal/romcal
- **Best for:** Generating a full Catholic calendar offline or in an app.
