# Catholic — Liturgy of the Hours (Breviarium)

Sources for the Roman Liturgy of the Hours and related texts.

---

## Breviarium-app / breviarium--core
- **What it is:** Open-source core of the Catholic Liturgy of the Hours and Mass readings as structured JSON.
- **What you get:** Lauds, Vespers, Compline, and Mass readings organized by date.
- **Link:** https://github.com/Breviarium-app/breviarium--core
- **Best for:** Modern structured Catholic hours.

---

## aensley / liturgy-day
- **What it is:** REST API specifically for the four main hours of the Liturgy of the Hours.
- **What you get:** Daily office texts via a simple API.
- **Link:** https://github.com/aensley/liturgy-day
- **Documentation:** https://liturgy.day/docs
- **Best for:** Quick API access to the Hours.

---

## DivinumOfficium / divinum-officium
- **What it is:** The largest open database of the traditional Roman Breviary.
- **What you get:** Full traditional office texts (raw files in `web/www/horas/`).
- **Link:** https://github.com/DivinumOfficium/divinum-officium
- **Best for:** Traditional (pre-Vatican II) Roman Breviary.

---

## Other useful Catholic sources
- **Catechism of the Catholic Church (CCC):** Various JSON versions exist on GitHub (search “catechism-ccc-json”).
- **Code of Canon Law 1983:** Structured versions available (search “canon law json” or “catebot”).
