## Oecumenisch Patriarchaat van Constantinopel
- **What it is:** The primus inter pares of the Eastern Orthodox Church.
- **Full list:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ecumenical_patriarchs_of_Constantinople
- **Notable figures:**
  - St. Gregory of Nazianzus (379–381) — Great theologian, one of the Cappadocian Fathers.
  - St. John Chrysostom (398–404) — Greatest preacher of the early Church; exiled.
  - St. Photius the Great (858–867 & 877–886) — Scholar and defender of Orthodoxy.
  - St. Tarasius (784–806) — Restored icons at the Second Council of Nicaea.
  - Bartholomew I (since 1991) — Current Ecumenical Patriarch.
