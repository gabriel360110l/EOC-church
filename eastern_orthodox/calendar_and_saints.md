# Eastern Orthodox — Calendar & Saints

---

## Orthocal API
- **What it is:** The best free daily source for Eastern Orthodox calendar data.
- **What you get:** Saints of the day with stories, troparia, readings, and fasting rules.
- **Link:** https://orthocal.info/api/gregorian/
- **Example:** `https://orthocal.info/api/gregorian/2026/10/17/`
- **Best for:** “Saint of the Day” and daily readings features.

---

## orthocal-service (full database)
- **What it is:** The backend database of Orthocal.
- **What you get:** Complete offline dataset of saints, readings, and calendar rules.
- **Link:** https://github.com/brianglass/orthocal-service
- **Best for:** Building a fully offline calendar.
