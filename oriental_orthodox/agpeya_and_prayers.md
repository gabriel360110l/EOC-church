# Oriental Orthodox — Agpeya & Prayers (Coptic & Syriac)

Sources for the Coptic Agpeya (Book of Hours) and related Oriental Orthodox prayer texts.

---

## api.coptic.io — Agpeya
- **What it is:** Free API that returns the full text of the Coptic Agpeya hours.
- **What you get:** Complete prayers, psalms, and structure for Prime, Terce, Sext, None, Vespers, Compline, Midnight, and the Veil Prayer.
- **Example endpoint:** `https://api.coptic.io/api/agpeya/prime`
- **Best for:** Live or cached Agpeya content in an app.

---

## abanoubha / agpeya (GitHub)
- **What it is:** Open-source repository with the Agpeya texts.
- **What you get:** Structured prayer texts that can be used offline.
- **Link:** Search GitHub for “abanoubha agpeya” or related Coptic prayer repos.
- **Best for:** Offline Agpeya data.

---

## Coptic Scriptorium
- **What it is:** Academic corpus of Coptic texts, including canons and writings of Athanasius, Shenoute, etc.
- **What you get:** High-quality Coptic and English texts of important Coptic church documents.
- **Link:** https://github.com/CopticScriptorium/corpora
- **Best for:** Deeper Coptic sources and canons.

---

## Syriac sources (Shhimo & others)
- **What it is:** Resources for the Syriac Orthodox prayer cycle (Shhimo) and related texts.
- **Useful links:**
  - https://github.com/srophe/srophe-app-data (persons, texts, and biographical data)
  - Peshitta projects on GitHub (search “Peshitta” or “ETCBC/peshitta”)
- **Best for:** Syriac Orthodox content.
