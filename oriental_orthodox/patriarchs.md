# Oriëntaals-Orthodoxe Patriarchen & Pausen

---

## Koptisch-Orthodoxe Paus van Alexandrië
- **What it is:** Successor of St. Mark the Evangelist. Head of the Coptic Orthodox Church.
- **Full list:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Coptic_Orthodox_popes
- **Notable figures:**
  - St. Mark the Evangelist (c. 43–68) — Founder of the Church in Egypt.
  - St. Athanasius the Great (328–373) — Defender of the Nicene faith against Arianism.
  - St. Cyril of Alexandria (412–444) — Champion of the title Theotokos.
  - St. Dioscorus I (444–454) — Last pope before the Chalcedonian schism.
  - Pope Kyrillos VI (1959–1971) — Modern saint known for miracles and monastic life.
  - Pope Shenouda III (1971–2012) — Long-serving pope, theologian and preacher.
  - Pope Tawadros II (since 2012) — Current pope.

---

## Syrisch-Orthodoxe Patriarch van Antiochië
- **What it is:** Successor in the line of St. Peter and St. Severus of Antioch.
- **Full list:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Syriac_Orthodox_patriarchs_of_Antioch
- **Notable figures:**
  - St. Ignatius of Antioch (c. 68–107) — Early martyr and theologian.
  - St. Severus of Antioch (512–538) — Greatest theologian of the non-Chalcedonian tradition.
  - Current: Ignatius Aphrem II (since 2014).
