# Oriental Orthodox — Calendar & Synaxarium

---

## api.coptic.io — Synaxarium
- **What it is:** Free API for the Coptic Synaxarium (lives of the saints).
- **What you get:** Saints and martyrs of the day with full English (and often Arabic) descriptions.
- **Endpoint example:** `https://api.coptic.io/api/synaxarium/2026-10-17`
- **Best for:** Daily Coptic saint content.

---

## @coptic/data & related packages
- **What it is:** Structured data packages used by Coptic Orthodox apps and sites.
- **What you get:** Synaxarium, readings (Katameros), and other liturgical data.
- **Search:** GitHub for “coptic data” or “katameros”.
- **Best for:** Offline Coptic calendar data.

---

## srophe / srophe-app-data (Syriac)
- **What it is:** Large dataset of Syriac persons, saints (Qadishe), and related information.
- **What you get:** Biographical and feast-day data for Syriac Orthodox saints.
- **Link:** https://github.com/srophe/srophe-app-data
- **Best for:** Syriac Orthodox calendar and saints.
