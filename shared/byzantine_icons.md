# Byzantine Style Icons

Byzantine iconography is the classic style used across Eastern Orthodox churches and highly appreciated for its beauty, theology, and coverage of nearly all saints.

---

## Wikimedia Commons — Byzantine & Orthodox Icons
- **What it is:** Large collection of high-quality photos of real Byzantine and post-Byzantine icons from museums and churches.
- **What you get:** Public domain or freely usable images of Christ Pantokrator, Theotokos, major saints, and feast icons.
- **Links:**
  - Category: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Byzantine_icons
  - Museum of Byzantine Culture (Thessaloniki): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Icons_in_Museum_of_Byzantine_Culture_in_Thessaloniki
- **Best for:** App icons, “Saint of the Day” images, or educational use. Always check the individual license.

---

## Picryl — Public Domain Orthodox Icons
- **What it is:** Search engine for public domain images, including many Byzantine-style icons.
- **What you get:** Free-to-use high-resolution images of classic icons.
- **Link:** https://picryl.com/topics/orthodox+faith/orthodox+icons
- **Best for:** Safe, license-free icon images.

---

## OrthodoxPhotos.com
- **What it is:** Website with free Orthodox icon collections and screen savers.
- **What you get:** Sets of icons of Christ, the Theotokos, and saints from Mount Athos, Meteora, and other Orthodox sources.
- **Link:** https://orthodoxphotos.com/screensavers.shtml
- **Best for:** Ready-made collections of beautiful icons.

---

## Notes
- Byzantine style is the standard for Eastern Orthodox and is also widely used and loved in other traditions.
- For an app: download the images you need, check licenses, and bundle them locally.
- Avoid modern copyrighted icon paintings unless permission is granted.
