# Shared Councils & Canons (First Three Ecumenical Councils)

These sources cover the councils and canons accepted by all three traditions (Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, and Catholic).

---

## NPNF2-14: The Seven Ecumenical Councils (XML)
- **What it is:** The classic English edition of the first seven ecumenical councils with all canons, creeds, and acts. Published by CCEL.
- **What you get:** Full text of the 85 Apostolic Canons, Nicaea I (325), Constantinople I (381), Ephesus (431), Chalcedon (451), and later councils, plus early local synods (Ancyra, Gangra, Antioch, Laodicea, etc.).
- **Link:** https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf214.xml
- **How to use:** Download once and filter:
  - Oriental Orthodox → keep only Apostolic Canons + Councils 1–3 + pre-451 local synods
  - Eastern Orthodox & Catholic → use the full file
- **Best for:** Reliable English text of the early canons and creeds.

---

## HistoricalChristianFaith / Writings-Database
- **What it is:** Structured GitHub database of early Christian writings, including council texts and apostolic documents.
- **What you get:** Parsed texts of the Didache, Apostolic Constitutions, council documents, and more.
- **Link:** https://github.com/HistoricalChristianFaith/Writings-Database
- **Best for:** Machine-readable early church documents.

---

## New Advent — Fathers & Councils
- **What it is:** Clean HTML pages of all the early councils and canons, numbered and easy to scrape or read.
- **What you get:** Individual pages for each canon (example: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3801.htm for Nicaea).
- **Base:** https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/
- **Best for:** Human-readable canons with traditional numbering.

---

## BenjaminPoole / Ecumenical-Christian-Library (Obsidian Vault)
- **What it is:** Large collection of Markdown files covering councils, creeds, and confessions from 325 onward.
- **What you get:** Over 1,000 ready-to-use Markdown files of councils and creeds.
- **Link:** https://github.com/BenjaminPoole/Ecumenical-Christian-Library-Obsidian-Vault
- **Best for:** Easy conversion to JSON or direct use in note-based apps.
