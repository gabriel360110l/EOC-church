# Geloofsbelijdenissen (Creeds)

The most important shared and tradition-specific creeds.

---

## Nicea-Constantinopel (381) — Gedeelde basis
- **What it is:** The Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed, accepted by Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, and Catholic churches.
- **What you get:** The full text of the Creed as used in the liturgy.
- **Key point:** The Catholic version includes the *Filioque* (“and the Son”). Eastern and Oriental Orthodox use the original text without Filioque.
- **Sources:**
  - NPNF / New Advent: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3801.htm (related council documents)
  - Many prayer books and the NPNF214.xml file listed in the councils section.
- **Best for:** Showing the common faith and the one major difference.

---

## Apostolische Geloofsbelijdenis (Apostles’ Creed)
- **What it is:** Shorter Western creed, widely used in Catholic and some other Western contexts.
- **What you get:** Simple, early summary of the faith.
- **Best for:** Catholic or Western-oriented sections of an app.

---

## Oriental Orthodox variations
- **What it is:** The same Nicene Creed, often with slight liturgical wording differences (especially in the Trisagion or surrounding prayers).
- **Best for:** Accurate display in Coptic or Syriac contexts.

---

## Practical tip
Store the original Greek/Latin text + Dutch and English translations in your app. Mark the Filioque clearly when showing the Catholic version.
