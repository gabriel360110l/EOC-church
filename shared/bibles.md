# Shared Bibles (Public Domain / Open)

These sources provide Bible texts that are useful across Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, and Catholic traditions. Most include the Deuterocanonical books.

---

## World English Bible (WEB) — Catholic & Orthodox Edition
- **What it is:** Modern English translation in the public domain. Includes the full Orthodox and Catholic canon (81 books, Psalm 151, 1–4 Maccabees, etc.).
- **What you get:** Clean modern English text of the entire Bible, ready for apps.
- **Links:**
  - JSON API: https://bible.helloao.org/api/ENGWEBP/books.json
  - USFM download: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/engwebp_usfm.zip
- **Best for:** English Bible text in any of the three traditions.

---

## Statenvertaling 1637 + Apocrypha (Dutch)
- **What it is:** Classic Dutch Statenvertaling including the Deuterocanonical books. Public domain.
- **What you get:** Full Dutch Bible text with Apocrypha.
- **Link:** https://github.com/Renerick/Statenvertaling-1637-plus-Apocriefen
- **Best for:** Dutch users who want a traditional Protestant-style text that also includes the extra books.

---

## Petrus Canisius Translation (Dutch Catholic)
- **What it is:** Classic Dutch Catholic Bible (1939) with the full 73-book canon. Public domain.
- **What you get:** Traditional Dutch Catholic Bible text.
- **Links:**
  - GitHub: https://github.com/Critical-Text/Petrus-Canisius-Vertaling
  - Wikisource: https://nl.wikisource.org/wiki/Petrus_Canisiusvertaling
- **Best for:** Dutch Catholic / shared use.

---

## scrollmapper / bible_databases
- **What it is:** Large collection of Bible texts in SQLite, JSON, and CSV formats.
- **What you get:** Douay-Rheims (Catholic), King James + Apocrypha, Brenton Septuagint (Orthodox OT), and more.
- **Link:** https://github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases
- **Best for:** Easy local databases or quick conversion to JSON.

---

## Brenton Septuagint (LXX)
- **What it is:** Classic English translation of the Greek Old Testament used by Orthodox churches.
- **What you get:** English text of the Septuagint (including books not in the Protestant canon).
- **Often found in:** scrollmapper/bible_databases or other LXX projects.
- **Best for:** Eastern & Oriental Orthodox Old Testament.

---

## Direct Dutch JSON (bolls.life)
- **What it is:** Ready-to-download full Bible JSON files.
- **What you get:** Complete Dutch Bible as one JSON file. Also DRC.json and LXXE.json available.
- **Link:** https://bolls.life/static/translations/NLD.json
- **Best for:** Quick Dutch Bible data.
