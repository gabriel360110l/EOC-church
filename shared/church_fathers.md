# Shared Church Fathers (especially pre-451)

These sources give access to the writings and Bible commentaries of the Church Fathers accepted by all three traditions (especially those who lived before the Council of Chalcedon in 451).

---

## HistoricalChristianFaith / Commentaries-Database
- **What it is:** The database behind CatenaBible.com. One JSON file per Bible verse containing commentaries from the Fathers.
- **What you get:** Verse-by-verse patristic commentary (Chrysostom, Athanasius, Cyril of Alexandria, Ephrem, Augustine, Basil, etc.). Also covers the Deuterocanonical books.
- **Link:** https://github.com/HistoricalChristianFaith/Commentaries-Database
- **Example path:** `jn/1/1.json` for John 1:1
- **Best for:** Linking Bible verses to Church Father quotes in an app.
- **Note:** Very large repository. Download only the books you need.

---

## HistoricalChristianFaith / Writings-Database
- **What it is:** Full texts of the writings, homilies, and letters of the Fathers, linked to the commentaries database.
- **What you get:** Complete chapters and works so users can go from a short quote to the full text.
- **Link:** https://github.com/HistoricalChristianFaith/Writings-Database
- **Best for:** Full context of patristic quotations.

---

## CCEL — Ante-Nicene & Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers (ANF & NPNF)
- **What it is:** The classic 38-volume English collection of the Church Fathers.
- **What you get:** Full English texts of almost all major early Fathers.
- **How to access:** Add `.xml` to any book code, e.g. https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf110.xml (Chrysostom on Matthew).
- **Base:** https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff
- **Best for:** Complete books and homilies.

---

## farag-opt / Catena-Bible
- **What it is:** Open-source project by Coptic Orthodox developers that structures patristic commentaries per verse.
- **What you get:** Another structured source of verse-level Father quotes, useful especially for Oriental Orthodox focus.
- **Link:** https://github.com/farag-opt/Catena-Bible
- **Best for:** Additional or alternative commentary data.

---

## openpatristics / patristic-citations
- **What it is:** Project that maps exactly which Bible verses the early Fathers quote.
- **What you get:** Cross-reference data between Fathers and Scripture (OT, LXX, NT).
- **Link:** https://github.com/openpatristics/patristic-citations
- **Best for:** Building “where is this verse quoted?” features.
